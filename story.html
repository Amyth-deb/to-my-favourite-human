How You Became My Favourite Human

It started in a study group. I had no idea it was the start of anything.

After a while we became Instagram friends. Then came our first WhatsApp conversation, which was about babysitting. Not exactly the beginning of a great story, but that is how it began, and I still find it funny.

From there it kept spreading. Instagram, WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, Messenger. I’m not sure how we ended up on all of them, but somewhere along the way, it became normal to find you everywhere.

You became my second female friend ever. That means something to me, because it didn’t happen often.

And somehow, you became my favourite person.

You helped me become more social. You were around during a difficult period of my life, and it mattered more than you probably know. I’m grateful for that. What I do with it is mine to carry, not yours, and I never want you to feel otherwise.

I trust you with things I don’t usually share with everyone. And I genuinely get happy when a message from you arrives. It’s a small thing, but it’s true.

We don’t agree on everything. We’ve had disagreements, and in some of them I struggled with jealousy and insecurity. That was mine to deal with, and I’m learning to handle those feelings better. None of it has made this friendship mean less to me.

I hope that one day we get to meet offline. I’ve imagined it many times. There’s no rush and no pressure. Just a hope, for whenever it works out.

That’s the story so far. There’s also a letter.
