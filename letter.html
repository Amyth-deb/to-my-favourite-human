A Letter For You

Dear Marisha,

Happy birthday.

I’ve written this in my head a few times and it never came out right, so I’m going to say it plainly.

You’re funny, and often savage about it. You’ll say something so sharp that I need a moment to recover. But underneath that you’re caring and soft, in a way you don’t advertise. I’ve noticed anyway.

You’re mature, beautiful and academically strong, and you have a great sense of fashion. You cook, you go to the gym. It’s honestly a bit unfair for one person.

I know you sometimes call yourself fat, or decide you look bad in back-camera photos. I won’t argue with your camera. But I genuinely think you’re beautiful, and I’m not saying it to be nice.

On 18 December we both have our medical admission exam. I sincerely hope you get into medical college and become a doctor. Whatever happens, I wish you success, happiness and everything you work so hard for. You’ve earned a good outcome.

I’d like our friendship to last a lifetime. I consider you part of my family. You don’t owe me anything for that, not a reply and not a promise. I just wanted you to know it.

Also, Telapokar Dhn. You know exactly what I mean. Some jokes don’t need explaining.

And since every good thing needs an organisation, I’m starting the Marisha Fan Club. I’m the president. Membership is currently one person, but the president is extremely dedicated.

Happy birthday, Marisha.

Your friend
