Why Marisha deserves a fan club

The club’s case, in eight points. The president declined to hire a lawyer.

  1. Funny

    Reliably, and without trying very hard. This is the main reason the club exists.

  2. Savage on the outside, soft inside

    The sharp edge is real. So is the caring, soft centre underneath it. The club has seen both and respects both.

  3. Mature

    Quietly, in a way that makes the president feel slightly underqualified for the office.

  4. Beautiful

    Stated plainly, for the record. The club will not be taking questions.

  5. Academically strong

    Serious about her work and good at it. The club finds this admirable and a little inconvenient.

  6. Excellent fashion sense

    The president has no standing to judge fashion, but is confident enough to say it anyway.

  7. Can cook

    A real skill, and the kind of thing that earns instant respect from any serious organisation.

  8. Goes to the gym

    The club admires this wholeheartedly, from a safe and sedentary distance.

The President’s Statement

I take this club very seriously. I have no authority, no funding, no office and no other members. I do have a title, a lot of enthusiasm and a firm belief that this is the best-run organisation of its size.

Meetings are held whenever I think of her, which is often.
